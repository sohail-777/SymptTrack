About SymptTrack

SymptTrack is a friendly pocket health journal that helps you log daily symptoms, get instant AI-assisted diagnosis suggestions, and understand your health trends over time — all in one calm, clinic-inspired space.

🎯

Our Mission

Most people only think about their health when something goes wrong. SymptTrack encourages small, daily check-ins so patterns become visible long before they become problems — and gives you a clear record to bring to your doctor.

🧠

The Model

Diagnosis suggestions are powered by CORe — Clinical Diagnosis Prediction, a transformer model trained on clinical notes to predict likely conditions from symptom text. If the model is temporarily unavailable, a transparent rule-based engine steps in automatically so you're never left without a result.

⚠️

Important Disclaimer

SymptTrack is a self-tracking and decision-support tool. It does not provide a medical diagnosis and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider with questions about a medical condition, and seek emergency care for urgent symptoms.
